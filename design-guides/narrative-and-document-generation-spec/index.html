    Narrative and document generation — specification¶

    Status: normative. Every change that plans a clinical document, adds a section, words a clinical fact in a narrative, or prepares facts for the LLM follows this document. A change that cannot follow it updates this document in the same PR, with the reason.

    Japanese counterpart: narrative-and-document-generation-spec.ja.md.

    Related specs and contracts: - Document type codes (LOINC per document type) and every display name: code-mapping-and-localization-spec.md §4.1. - Two-pass CIF (structural stubs, then narratives; AD-65): clinosim/modules/output/SPEC.md. - Determinism of the narrative pass (walk order, timestamps): determinism-and-rng-spec.md §7.

    1. Principles¶

    1. One wording per fact. A clinical fact read into prose is worded by one function, and the template path and the LLM path both call it. The two paths differ in what they do with the words: the template places them, the LLM receives them as facts. They never word the fact differently.
    2. One decision per document. Which documents an encounter gets is decided by one table of planners keyed by the document type's generation frequency. Each Composition section has one registered builder.
    3. The display and its interpretation agree. A value is classified as it is shown (rounded first), so a printed number never contradicts the band or interpretation printed beside it.
    4. Every rule has a guard. Each rule names the test that enforces it; a rule without one is listed in §5.

The skin item states the Braden risk band (YAML braden_bands) at or below braden_report_at_or_below with skin observation, and repositioning and pressure relief at or below braden_prevention_at_or_below. The mobility item states the Barthel total with its band and the help level of mobility and toileting from the ADL assessment in force. The surgical-site item states the post-operative day after the encounter's surgical procedure (SNOMED surgical category) with the routine observation, no finding, up to the site_observation limit of its aftercare category (Issue #3556: a vascular-access procedure such as PCI gets a puncture-site line (bleeding, hematoma, distal pulses) for max_days 1 and a device implant a pocket line for 3 days, never a post-operative day; the limits are modelling choices).     3. Wording clinical facts¶

    Rule Canonical code Guard
    An inpatient note cites the abnormal lab results its writer knew: those reported at or before its authored time — for a daily note, those of the window_hours up to it (reference_data/notable_labs.yaml, since the previous day's note, so a result reported after one note is cited by the next; without an authored time, its hospital day), the whole record for a stay-level document. It lists each analyte once — the day's worst draw (panic flag, then hemolysed specimen H*, then H / L) at the analyte's reported precision, with the previous value — ranked panic results, then results naming an abnormality (per-analyte findings of reference_data/notable_labs.yaml, KDIGO 2012 AKI by creatinine rise, hemolysed specimen), then newly abnormal, then persisting ones, and counts the analytes beyond the cap. The template Assessment and the LLM context (abnormal_labs_today / abnormal_labs_during_stay) word them through the same function. A complication appears once its onset time has passed (new when it began within the same window, ongoing afterwards); an entry without an onset day is left out of a day-scoped note. clinosim/modules/document/narrative/lab_timeseries.py::select_notable_labs, _notable_labs.py::notable_lab_entries, clinosim/modules/observation/engine.py::format_lab_value, renderers/_composers.py::_complications_known_on_day tests/unit/modules/document/narrative/test_progress_assessment_known_at_note_time_3229_3230.py
    A daily progress note's Assessment leads with the problem of the stay: the admission diagnosis and the working diagnoses present from admission (onset_day 0), coded and named, with the hospital day; never the discharge diagnosis or a diagnosis of a later onset (renderers/_composers.py::_working_diagnosis_line, phrase progress.working_diagnosis).     A daily progress note's plan names the drugs of its period — the window_hours up to its authored time (reference_data/notable_labs.yaml), or its hospital day without one — by what happened to them, compared with the period before: started (first given in the period, from the second period of the stay), continued, given once (a dose of an order with a one-time frequency or marked single-dose, never "continued"), held (with the reason; a hold goes on until a dose is given, so a drug whose latest earlier dose was held stays held, not stopped), resumed (given now after its latest earlier dose was held), stopped (given in the previous period and not again, unless only as needed).
